BODYWEIGHT TRACKER - install as an Android app

1. Upload this whole folder (or the unzipped files) to a free static host:
   - Netlify Drop: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drop the folder/files, or
   - GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, or any host that serves https.
   Keep the files together: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and the three icon-*.png files.
2. Open the address you get in Chrome on your Android phone.
3. Tap the three dots menu, then "Install app" (not just "Add to Home screen").
4. Open it from your app drawer or home screen. It opens full screen with no Chrome tabs.

MOVING YOUR OLD LOG
Your log lives in the browser where it was made, so the installed app starts empty.
In the Claude version: Progress > Export backup > Copy.
In the installed app: Progress > Restore backup > paste > Replace my data.
